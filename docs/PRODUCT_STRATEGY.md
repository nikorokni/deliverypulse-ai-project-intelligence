# Product Strategy

## Vision

Help delivery leaders make timely, evidence-based trade-offs before a release becomes late.

## Target users

1. **Product Manager** — needs to balance customer value, scope and target dates.
2. **Project / Delivery Manager** — needs early warning of blockers, dependencies and overloaded teams.
3. **Engineering Manager** — needs a credible capacity view and a way to explain delivery risk.
4. **Executive sponsor** — needs a concise answer to “will we ship, and what decision is required?”

## Jobs to be done

- When a release is approaching, show whether the current plan remains credible.
- When confidence falls, explain which constraints caused the change.
- When stakeholders disagree, compare scope and schedule scenarios using the same measures.
- When risk requires escalation, make ownership and due dates explicit.

## Product principles

- **Decision before reporting:** every insight should support an action.
- **Explainable confidence:** never produce a score without its contributing signals.
- **Outcomes over activity:** velocity is context, not the goal.
- **One portfolio language:** product, delivery and engineering use the same definitions.

## North Star

**Percentage of at-risk releases where a mitigation decision is recorded before the final two sprints.**

## MVP outcomes

- Reduce time spent assembling weekly status reports.
- Increase early identification of release risk.
- Improve scope and date decision quality.
- Establish consistent risk ownership across workstreams.
